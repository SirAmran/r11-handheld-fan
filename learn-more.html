About This Fan

The R11 Handheld Fan is a compact, rechargeable fan built for quick, strong airflow wherever you need to cool down. A quick double-click on the side button turns it on; double-click again to switch it off.

It's light enough to carry in one hand — for your desk, your bag, or on the move — and comes with a digital display so you can see your speed setting at a glance.
